# wet: Stream Flow and Water Temperature for the BC Freshwater Atlas

Mean monthly and annual stream flow for the British Columbia Freshwater
Atlas (FWA), published openly per stream segment from two sources: the
Pacific Climate Impacts Consortium's (PCIC) routed hydrologic model, and
an open water balance fitted at Water Survey of Canada (HYDAT) gauges.
Daily flow and water temperature at hydrometric stations are summarised
per year over month-day windows, so each year can be compared with
normal. Under active development.
