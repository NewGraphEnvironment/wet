## Outcome

#130 put the Maxan Creek pilot (#120) and its snow regression (#121) in front of an outside reader as a pkgdown article, `vignettes/short-record-flow.Rmd` ("Chinook Flow at a Short-Record Stream"). Maxan was gauged for five summers (1975–1979); the article shows its window flows filled for 1931–2024 from nearby gauges, the change from 1931–1980 to 2011–2024, how far each filling route can be trusted, and how the window flows follow each year's snow. `scripts/departure_snow.R` now saves its series after its report, as the pilot does; `data-raw/short_record_vignette_data.R` bundles the article's numbers from the two saved series, each held to its line in the tracked report, and `data-raw/short_record_vignette_map.R` its map. At the plan gate the article kept #120's nearest Bulkley donors for its second estimate rather than #122's covariance-picked ones. The plan review renamed it from "ungauged" to "short-record", since the main route needs Maxan's five years. What the article says is in its text; what is known is in `research/station_flow_departure.md`, which now links it.

## Measurement

- Maxan Creek, typical year 2011–2024 against 1931–1980: spawning-window flow −56 % (90 % range −73 % to −28 %), its lowest seven days −65 % (−78 % to −43 %), migration-window flow −14 % (−33 % to +9 %). The borrowed departures, which reach 29–30 of the 50 years before 1981, give −46 % and −3 %.
- Snow, 2011–2025 against 1950–1980, on all three watersheds: half of each year's snowmelt about ten days earlier, the water in the peak snowpack 3–4 % lower. Migration-window flow follows both (about a quarter more flow per ten days' later half-melt, about a third more per 100 mm of peak snow water), spawning-window flow neither (each explains at most 6 % of its variation between years).
- The snow regression's report regenerated byte-identical with the new save; the data bundle reproduces `identical()`, and three mutations (a series file stamped with another md5, an uncommitted script, one report number moved by 0.2) each refuse.
- Review: the plan review (1 blocker, 9 gaps) and three code-check rounds found 20 defects, none in the scoring or the report parsing, all in what the article's prose and guards claimed: a sentence naming the method in an everyday word (average, nearest, long-record, straight-line, snow, record) whose guard pinned only the number. Round 3 enumerated all 65 published units; body prose ends at 599 words of a 600 cap.
