## The mechanism

Every round 1 finding has one cause. Each sentence describes the method in the words a reader would use for it ("average", "nearest", "the gauges it is filled from", "a third or more"), not by the quantity or set the code computes. Each guard then pins the interpolated number, never the word around it. So a guard checks the value against the bundle, and nothing checks the definition, set membership or quantifier the word claims (writing.md, "State each claim by the code's definition"; code-check.md, "An assertion that matches an interpolated value cannot see the claim around it"). Round 1 fixed four of these sentences. The same cause reaches the places below, and none of them has a guard that could see it.

## Findings

- **[severity: bug]** vignettes/short-record-flow.Rmd:316-317. "Hidden five years at a time at 21 gauges" has the test backwards. The pilot's test (`scripts/departure_pilot.R` header, "The test"; report section 2) cuts each long record into blocks of five consecutive years. Each block is **kept** as the gauge's observed record, and the gauge's complete years outside the block are filled and scored. That is 218 blocks and 11,773 held-out block-years, about 54 hidden years per block. A reader of "hidden five years at a time" pictures five years removed from a long record and filled back in, which is a much easier test than the one the 0.50 and 0.42 come from. It also undersells how close the test is to Maxan's own case of five years kept. Suggested wording: "Cut back to five years at a time at 21 gauges, with the other years filled and scored". No guard reads this sentence.

- **[severity: bug]** vignettes/short-record-flow.Rmd:321-322. "Each year's departure ... at the nearest long-record gauges" repeats round 1's "nearest gauge" error in the sibling bullet. Round 1's note said "the next bullet qualifies 'nearest in place and size'", but only the weighting is qualified there, not the selection. The carried donors are the k = 5 departure-pool gauges nearest in predictor space: easting, northing and log drainage area, each scaled (pilot header, "Donor availability per year"). For Maxan they are 08EE003, 08EE013, 08EE008, 08EE020 and 08JA016, the same five in both windows. The Bulkley at Quick (08EE004) is in the departure pool, is 55.5 km away and has the longest record (94 years), and it is not one of them. Telkwa (90.2 km) and MacIvor (63.3 km) are. "Long-record" is a proxy too. The pool's criterion is at least 10 complete years inside 1981–2010, and MacIvor Creek's whole record is 1977–1995, 16 migration years. The same proxy appears at line 310-311 ("by hiding gauges with long records"), whose departure pool includes gauges with 16 years. Suggested wording: "at the five gauges nearest in place and size with a 1981–2010 record, weighted toward the nearest of them".

- **[severity: bug]** vignettes/short-record-flow.Rmd:315-316. "through a straight-line fit between its flow and Maxan's" is false as written. The shipped `ols` fits log Maxan on log donor for the mean and min7 statistics (pilot header, candidate (a)). The point is the back-transformed median, so the relation between the flows themselves is a power law, not a straight line. This is the same "average" versus geometric-mean slip that round 1 fixed in the headline. Suggested wording: "a straight-line fit between the logs of its flow and Maxan's", or "a fit between its flow and Maxan's".

- **[severity: bug]** vignettes/short-record-flow.Rmd:329-331, with the guard at 94. "The borrowed departures give smaller drops, 3% lower ... and 46% lower ... but the same picture" reads as the same comparison by another route. It is not, so part of the difference may come from the years compared, not the route. The carried change compares 2011–2024 against the 29 (migration) or 30 (spawning) years before 1981 that have a carried value (`n_pre_carried`). Those are 1931, 1933, 1944–1951 and 1960 or 1961–1980. The answer's change uses all 50 years of 1931–1980. In 1931–1951 the carried value is 08EE003's departure alone, at weight 1.0, so like Quick in the extension those years carry the Bulkley's own history. The guard pins the signs and the relative sizes, and cannot see the period. Name the carried route's years ("against the 30 years before 1981 its donors cover"), or drop the comparison of sizes.

- **[severity: fragile]** vignettes/short-record-flow.Rmd:146-148. The two distance guards cannot fail. `abs(x - 10 * round(x / 10)) < 5` holds for every `x` except an exact `…5.0`, so neither "within about" is pinned. Rounding to the nearest 10 can also land below the true maximum: a farthest fill gauge at 74 km would print "within about 70 km". The `far_all` sentence names the gauges "that lend only their departures" but takes the maximum over every gauge. That is true today only because the farthest gauge, Telkwa, is a lend gauge. Use `ceiling(max / 10) * 10` (or `round()` with a guard `far >= max - 2`), and take `far_all` from `d_km[names(d_km) == "lend"]`. Then restore a value of 74 km and confirm the guard fires.

- **[severity: fragile]** vignettes/short-record-flow.Rmd:142-144, with tests/testthat/test-vignette_data.R:247. The map's roles come from `map$stations$extension_donor` and `carried_donor`, which are copies of `v$gauges` taken when the map was built. The article never compares them with the values bundle, and the test checks only the set of station numbers. Rebuilding the values bundle without rebuilding the map would draw the old fill/lend split under the new numbers. They agree today: fill is 08EE004, 08EE008 and 08EE013, and lend is 08EE003, 08EE020 and 08JA016, the same in both windows, so drawing one class per gauge is right. Take the roles from `v$gauges` by station number, or assert `identical()` on the two columns in the article or the test ("One fact derived twice").

- **[severity: fragile]** vignettes/short-record-flow.Rmd:311-312. "0 is no better than calling every year average" is the same word round 1 removed from the headline. Both scores are NSE on log values. For the fill, 0 is the observed years' mean log value. For the borrowed departures, 0 is "no departure", from both series centred on their log mean. In the article's own vocabulary that is "calling every year typical", or "calling every year normal" for the departures. Low impact, but it puts "average" back beside "typical year" for the same quantity.

- **[severity: fragile]** vignettes/short-record-flow.Rmd:379-380. "explains at most 6% of its year-to-year change" is an R² over the years' departure levels, which is variation from year to year, not change from one year to the next (and "change" elsewhere in the article means the before/after). Suggested wording: "of how much it varies from year to year".

- **[severity: fragile]** CLAUDE.md, the new short-record paragraph: "holds every bundled number to its line in the tracked pilot or snow report". Several bundled numbers are held to no report line: the gauges' `area_km2` (from HYDAT STATIONS), the carried donors' weights inside `carried$donors`, and `extended$donor`, which sets the article's quoted 31 of 50 and the map's fill class. `nums()` reads the donor's digits as v[5:6] and never checks them. The data script's own header says "Every number the article can quote", which is narrower and nearly right. Make CLAUDE.md say the same.
