## Findings

- **[bug] vignettes/segment-discharge.Rmd:100, 107-109; NEWS.md (new entry, line 2):** "On every one of the 9,000 segments in the Salmon River group", the Figure 1 caption "all 9,000 Salmon River segments", and NEWS "all 9,000 Salmon River (SALR) segments" are false. SALR has **9,384** segments (`fwa_stream_networks_sp`, queried); 9,000 is the count fwapg has a discharge row for. `research/fwapg_mad_method.md` already says 384 of 9,384 are absent from the lookup. The stopifnot (`n_par == prov$fwapg_discharge_rows[["SALR"]]`) pins "every segment fwapg has a value for", not "every segment in the group", so the claim can be wrong while it passes. Suggested wording: "every one of the 9,000 segments fwapg has a value for (of 9,384 in the group)".

- **[bug] vignettes/segment-discharge.Rmd:410-412 (limits bullet), and captions at 273-281:** "`8` of the order-`3` segments shown" renders as "8 of the order-3 segments shown". The 8 segments are of orders 3, 3, 5, 5, 6, 7, 7, 7 (DB: BULK 17046403, 17046241 order 3; 17094384 order 5; 17080016 order 6; 17055995, 17056177, 17048052 order 7, edge type 1250; SALR 702767507 order 5). They are also **not shown**: line 172 drops them before drawing. Three are order-7 pieces of the Bulkley mainstem, so the BULK map has small gaps in its largest river that the legend doesn't explain. For the same reason, the captions' "the 2,180 Salmon River segments of order 3 or more" and "the 7,748 Bulkley River segments of order 3 or more" count only the drawn segments. There are 2,181 and 7,755 segments of order 3 or more. Fix by saying "of order 3 or more" and "left off the maps", or by counting before the drop.

- **[bug] NEWS.md (new entry):** "At the five gauges nearest SALR the balance is high and PCIC low" is false. The five are the four calibration gauges within 30 km plus the outlet gauge 08KC001, which is 46.4 km away. Three other calibration gauges are nearer than that: 07EE009 at 39.2 km, 07EE010 at 40.4 km and 07ED003 at 44.3 km (from `skill` lon/lat against the SALR polygon). The research file words it correctly ("those within 30 km of SALR plus its outlet gauge 08KC001"); NEWS should match.

- **[fragile / overstated] vignettes/segment-discharge.Rmd:259, 287-290; research/water_balance_method.md (new bullet "both products miss, in opposite directions"):** "fwapg runs low" at all 5 gauges is pinned only by `all(ga$fw_err < 0)`, a sign test that −0.01 % would pass. The actual fwapg errors are −1 % (08JE004) and −2 % (08KC003), which is not a miss. At those two gauges only the balance is off (+34 %, +35 %). The bracket at 08KC001 (+38 % / −25 %) is real. But the research heading "both products miss, in opposite directions", and "So the gap belongs to both" as a conclusion drawn from all five gauges, claim more than the table shows. A magnitude threshold in the assertion (for example fwapg low by more than 5 % at each gauge), or wording that says fwapg is close at two of the five gauges, would make the claim and the check agree.

- **[fragile] vignettes/segment-discharge.Rmd:90, 402-404:** The intro's "the Bulkley River ..., where fwapg has no discharge" and the bullet's "the open water balance is the only estimate" are not pinned by any stopifnot. Line 305 prints `fwapg_discharge_rows[["BULK"]]` inline, so that sentence corrects itself. The intro and the bullet would go false silently if a rebuilt fwapg gained BULK rows. Add `stopifnot(prov$fwapg_discharge_rows[["BULK"]] == 0)`.

- **[fragile, low] vignettes/segment-discharge.Rmd:332, 346-347:** "Zone 24 ... is the outlier". The assertion pins `zone_mae[[worst]] > 2 * mae_all` but not that no other zone also clears that bar. It holds today: the next zones are 25 at 49.7 % and 17 at 47.5 %, against a bar of 55.4 %. A refit that pushed a second zone over the bar would leave "the outlier" (singular) wrong with every assertion passing. `sum(zone_mae > 2 * mae_all) == 1` would pin it.
