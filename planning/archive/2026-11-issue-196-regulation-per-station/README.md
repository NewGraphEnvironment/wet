## Outcome

Every station `wet_station_list()` lists outside HYDAT (211 provincial, 46 BC Water Tool) now carries a regulation status, natural, regulated or unknown, from what sits upstream of it, under a rule registered before it was applied (`scripts/station_regulation_rule.R`, registered at 2d93787f, three amendments at 0c6d31ff and 6c9cb1e5, each the owner's decision). `scripts/station_regulation.R` snaps each station on the stream its name or drainage area gives, places BC's dams and water rights licences upstream as #53 did, reads each segment's published mean annual flow from verified parts, and writes `inst/extdata/station_sources/regulation_20261028.csv`, which `wet_station_list()` reads through `wet_station_regulation()`. `wet_station_select()` stays HYDAT-only; a donor pool of the natural stations is #202. What was learned: the licence layer is "Access Only" (only computed values ship, never a licence record), and "natural" is the rule's fall-through, so every filter that drops evidence (groundwater rows counted as surface, no-volume licences, #53's 1 mm own-reach test, unplaced points, unchecked codes) fails toward natural unless it is counted, routed to unknown or stopped on; four review rounds found and closed each. Findings: `research/station_sources.md`, "Regulation per non-HYDAT station".

## History moved from research/station_sources.md (#207, 2026-11-25)

The research file now states only the current rule and counts; the passages below, as they stood there on 2026-11-25, record how they were reached.

- **Amendment 1, 2026-11-24, by the owner's choice, before any station was placed.** The first run stopped on #53's check that every POD a shared licence quantity is split over is counted: one licence splits its quantity over its diversion point and a rediversion point, which is never counted, so the diversion point carried half. A shared quantity is now split over the PODs that are not rediversions, since a rediversion moves water already diverted. No status exists under the registered version.
- **Amendment 2, 2026-11-24, by the owner's choice, after review and before any status shipped.** 1,646 Current surface licence rows that would count state no volume: 880 "Total Flow" (the whole flow of the source) and 766 a zero or missing quantity. They cannot enter the shares, so a station below one could read natural with its licensed use unknown; such a station is now unknown unless something else regulates it. The same review found the script counting groundwater points of diversion in the shares, against the rule's "surface"; that was corrected in the script. Under the registered rule with Amendment 1 alone, 92 stations had read natural; 17 of them are unknown under Amendment 2, and the surface correction makes 2 regulated stations natural (licensed use 44.5 % and 18.3 % with groundwater, 3.8 % and 9.1 % without).
- **Amendment 3, 2026-11-24, by the owner's choice, after review and before any status shipped.** #53's stream-position test dropped a point on the station's own reach that indexed even 1 mm below it, so a licence indexed exactly at a gauge (West Line Creek, 08NK0006: licensed use 36 % of its flow) did not count. A point on the station's own line within 100 m below it now counts as upstream: closer than a gauge's and a point of diversion's positions can be told apart. It moves 08NK0006 and 08HC0004 (a regulating dam 44 m below) from natural to regulated. Beyond the margin, John Frank Lake Dam sits 213 m below 08LE0001 on its line, which stays natural; the report lists each such dam. The report gives the statuses under each version since stations were placed: 94 natural under Amendment 1 alone (with the surface correction), 77 with Amendment 2, 75 with Amendment 3.
