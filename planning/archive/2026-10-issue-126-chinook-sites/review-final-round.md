## Findings

- **[severity: bug]** vignettes/articles/flow-change-sparse-gauges.Rmd:158-161 and 169. The guard checks a different point from the one the sentence names. "Naver Creek flows into the Fraser River at Hixon" is about the confluence. `km("Hixon", "naver") < 5` measures to `map$targets`, which for a gauged site is the HYDAT gauge (08KE014, 1.1 km from Hixon). Naver's FWA mouth (blue line 356363814, measure 0: -122.6606, 53.43096) is 5.4 km west of the Hixon feature, so the guard would fail if it measured the place the sentence names. "Near Topley" (mouth 17.6 km, gauge 18.4 km) and "at Valemount" (mouth 2.8 km, gauge 1.2 km) both hold at the mouth too, but by luck: their guards also read the gauge. Fix: have the map script record each stream's mouth (the blue line at `downstream_route_measure = 0`) and run `km()`/`compass()` on it. Then either say "near Hixon" or set the threshold so it holds at the mouth.

- **[severity: fragile]** vignettes/articles/flow-change-sparse-gauges.Rmd:166. Two claims rest on proxies:
  - "two in the Skeena system and two in the Fraser" is held only by the hand-set `map` column of `scripts/departure_sites.csv`. Gosnell's receiving chain stops at the Morice (`into2`), so nothing derived reaches the Skeena for it. Fix: assert the watershed-code prefix (400 for the Skeena, 100 for the Fraser) in the map script, or carry the code's first level onto `targets`.
  - "northern British Columbia" (also line 116, changed from "central") has no guard and is arguable. Swift Creek is at 52.8 N and Naver at 53.4 N, south of BC's mid-latitude, in the Robson Valley and the Cariboo. Either restore "central" or name the regions.

- **[severity: fragile]** vignettes/articles/flow-change-sparse-gauges.Rmd, `region()`: `rv <- s[s$gnis_name %in% unique(c(t$into1, t$into2)) & !s$main, ]`. Naver's `into2` is NA, and `NA %in% c(..., NA)` is TRUE. So on the Fraser map, the one unnamed order-7+ stream in `map$streams` (measured: fraser 1, skeena 3) joins `rv` as a river to label. Its NA label is dropped silently today (warnings are off), so nothing shows. But it takes a slot in `!duplicated()` and would print if the labelling changed. Fix: `stats::na.omit(unique(c(t$into1, t$into2)))`, or `!is.na(s$gnis_name) &`.
