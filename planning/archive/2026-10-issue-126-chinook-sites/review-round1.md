## Findings

- **[bug]** vignettes/articles/chinook-sites.Rmd:71 — the link `[Maxan Creek](../short-record-flow.html)` resolves to the site root and 404s. pkgdown 2.2.0's `package_vignettes()` keeps a vignette already under `articles/` where it is (`file_out[!path_has_parent(file_out, "articles")] <- path("articles", ...)`), so `vignettes/articles/chinook-sites.Rmd` is built to `articles/chinook-sites.html`, the same directory as `articles/short-record-flow.html`. `../short-record-flow.html` therefore points at `/wet/short-record-flow.html`, which does not exist. It should be `short-record-flow.html`.

- **[bug: wrong attribution in prose]** vignettes/articles/chinook-sites.Rmd:223-224 — "the published flow at Gosnell Creek is `wet`'s own". It is not: the normal is the `pick` source on the mouth segment, which is `pcic_routed` in both windows (data/checks/departure_site_gosnell_bulk_20260717.txt, "pick pcic_routed"; the bundle's `normal$pick`). It is PCIC's routed hydromosaic flow, which `wet` republishes, and the Data paragraph (lines 235-246) never cites PCIC. The repo's own rule (CLAUDE.md, "PCIC's routed flow is published, cited and labelled by source") requires the source to be named. The article should say the normal is PCIC's routed flow as published by `wet`, and cite PCIC beside the other data sources. Line 213, "`wet`'s published flow for the stream", is accurate as written.

- **[bug: claim the data do not support]** vignettes/articles/chinook-sites.Rmd:191-208 — "Spawning-window flow follows snow much less: snow accounts for at most 16% of its spread." The 16 % comes from the peak-SWE-only fit (`predictor == "swe"`) and nothing else. The snow reports also fit melt date, and it explains more of the spawning window: the doy-only R² is 0.23 at Gosnell and 0.23 at Swift, and the two-predictor fit reaches 0.25 at Swift (departure_site_snow_*_bulk_20260717.txt, lines 57-67). The melt-date slope's 90 % interval is above zero in the spawning window at Gosnell [0.081, 0.181], Swift [0.062, 0.132] and Naver [0.049, 0.248]. So "snow accounts for at most 16%" is true only of peak SWE. The guard `r2_spw < min(r2_mig) / 2` checks only the SWE fit as well, so it cannot see this. Either say "peak snow" in that sentence or take the bound over all three models.

- **[fragile: header claims more than the guard checks]** data-raw/chinook_sites_data.R:14-17 — the header says "Every number the article can quote is held to its tracked report". Several numbers the article quotes are never held:
  - `scores$ols`: the article's "Filled-in record" column, and the guard `sc$ols[swift] < 0.2`. The race line it holds prints only the maximum median and ratio's median. At Swift the maximum is ratio, so Swift's ols (0.14) is never checked, and no site's `move1` is.
  - `scores$ext_gauges`: the "Gauges tested" column and "only 11 gauges" in the prose.
  - `sites$obs_first` and `sites$obs_last`: the 1985–1998 and 1956–1975 in the prose.
  - `sites$km2`.

  Today these come from the same rds that printed the report, so they agree. But the guard's scope is narrower than its header, which is the shape that lets a later change pass. Separately, line 127 takes the HEADLINE's "not scored" count from the report it is checking, so that one number is held to itself.

- **[fragile: header/body]** scripts/departure_site.R:19-25 against the `saveRDS()` at the end of the loop — the header lists the rds contents as including `donors` ("the target's carried donors per statistic and window"). The body writes no `donors` element; the donors exist only inside `mx[[...]]$a$donors`. The body also writes `race` and `carried`, which the header does not list. Nothing reads `$donors` today, but the registered description of the artifact is wrong.

- **[fragile: header/body]** scripts/departure_site.R, the ungauged branch where `err` is built (`obs = if (length(o) >= 10) mean(o) else NA_real_`) — the normal's error requires at least 10 baseline window-years at a gauge. The header's rule ("the gauge's observed 1981-2010 mean of that window's complete years") states no floor. All 29 Gosnell gauges have at least 10 years, so nothing moves today, but the body adds a condition the registered rule does not have.

- **[fragile]** scripts/departure_site.R, the ungauged basin query (`SELECT sum(ST_Area(geom)) / 1e6 km2 FROM whse_basemapping.fwa_watersheds_poly WHERE wscode_ltree <@ ...`) — a float sum over rows in no fixed order. `km2` goes into the target's predictor space through `stations_t`, so it feeds the donor weights and anomalies, while the header promises that a rerun "reproduces it byte for byte". In practice the difference is last-bit and would change printed output only at a rounding tie (code-check-r.md, "Postgres row order", second shape). An `ORDER BY` in a subquery, or `round()` before use, removes it.

- **[fragile: prose misstates a measurement]** research/chinook_sites.md:36 — "In the upper Fraser box, most of it east of -118.5° and in Alberta". The box spans -121.17 to -117.37, so only about 30 % of it lies east of -118.5°. planning/active/findings.md had the right sense ("most of the box east of about -118.5 is Alberta"), and the research file reversed it while compressing it.
