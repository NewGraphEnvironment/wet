## Findings

- **[severity: bug]** scripts/wb_aet_compare.R:252 (fed by scripts/wb_province.R:136-139). The tracked report says the opposite of what the count is. `n_m16[[1]]` is `sum(fr == 0)`, the cells with **no** MOD16 cover (filled wholly with cfu). wb_province.R writes it as `aet_mod16 full N`, which fits ex_filled_cells.txt's "cells filled" meaning. wb_aet_compare.R then prints it as `MOD16 cover on the analysis grid: full 137890; partial 179665; gap_cell_equivalents 208815.4`, and a reader takes "cover ... full 137890" to mean 137,890 cells fully covered by MOD16. I measured the smoke run's layers.tif (data/wb/711a9d102f, read only): 2,442,250 unmasked cells, of which 137,890 have frac 0, 2,124,695 have frac 1 and 179,665 are partial. The number printed as "full" is really the gap count, about 16x smaller than the actual full-cover count. This line goes into data/checks/wb_aet_compare.txt and will likely be quoted in research/water_balance_method.md §0 (Phase 4). The fix is to relabel it in the report (for example "MOD16 gap fill on the analysis grid (cells): wholly cfu N; part cfu N; cfu cell-equivalents N"), in the province file's labels, or in both.
