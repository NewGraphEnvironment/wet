## Mechanism

The prose uses a plain word for a property, such as "complete", "the window's mean flow", "window-years", "at Houston" or "under the mean". The word's real meaning is set somewhere the sentence does not show: a function default (`min_frac = 0.8`), a counting unit that changes between two sentences, a distance threshold chosen to pass, or a rounding step applied after the guard. The enumeration in `enumeration-27.md` indexes `` `r …` `` expressions and captions, so it only sees a qualifier when the qualifier sits next to a computed value. A qualifier carried by a typed noun is outside its candidate set, and so is the same number restated in NEWS.md or research/. Round 1's fix introduced "complete window", which is exactly that kind of noun. So the r1 to r3 chain has one root: words that sound exact, backed by computations that enforce something looser.
