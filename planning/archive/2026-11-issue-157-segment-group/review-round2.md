## Findings

- **[severity: bug]** research/segment_group.md:28 (and the label it was read from, scripts/segment_group_closure.R:93 → data/checks/segment_group_closure.txt "groups whose closure is a whole basin") — "72 groups of any size have a whole top-level drainage as their closure", added by round 1's fix, is not what the code counts. `whole = any(nchar(x$roots) == 3L)` (segment_group_closure.R:56) is true when the closure *contains* a whole drainage, and for many of the 72 the closure is a whole drainage plus parts of others. From the tracked table, 63 groups have a three-character first root and 33 of those span more than one code: ALBN, BRKS, CLAY, GOLD, HOLB, SANJ, TAHS (930 whole plus hundreds of roots under 935), LFRA (100 plus 900, 905, 970), LSKE (400 plus 910, 915), UFRA (100 plus 999), the twelve 910/915 groups, the 900/905 groups and others. So at least 33 of the 72 do not "have a whole top-level drainage as their closure"; they hold one. This is the round-1 defect one axis over: the corrected 48 sentence gained a new quantified claim that was not checked against the producer. Fix: "72 groups' closures include a whole top-level drainage" (and the same wording in the report label).
  - **Rule:** code-check.md "A guard's scope, escape hatches, and remedies"

- **[severity: bug]** research/segment_group.md:32 and :49 — two capability claims the script does not have. Line 32: "Where the province run's samples exist the build reads them, so only a group whose inputs changed is sampled again." segment_group.R:181 samples a group only when `<key>/sample/<G>.rds` is missing (or `WET_WB_RESAMPLE=1` is set); nothing compares inputs. Line 49: "it can build a scenario from new input layers for that group, on a machine holding them." New input layers mean a new province key, and the script refuses that run before it samples anything: `wb_key_dir()` (wb_fit_lib.R:43-47) stops unless exactly one complete province run exists, line 62 refuses any release but the shipped one, line 67 refuses a fit made under other scoring code, and line 105 stops unless the live build's balance was made from `basename(key_dir)`. So with changed inputs no group is "sampled again" and no scenario is built: the run stops. Either cut the two clauses or say what would have to change (a mode that skips the live-build guard and the parity section).
