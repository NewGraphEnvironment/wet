## The mechanism

A count or a label in the report (or research) is printed with a noun written from the intended design (the registered rule, the name of the set) while the number beside it is computed from a different object: the scored rows, a constant, a first-last span, one statistic. The number is checked; the noun is not. Rounds 1 and 2 were the same: "218 cut" (cut vs covered), "every pre-1981 year" (pre-1981 vs pre-1961), "the only donor reaching" (donors that supplied vs donors that reach). This is `code-check.md` "An assertion that matches an interpolated value cannot see the claim around it" (fly#99 row: "the number was right and the noun was not") and "One fact derived twice" (a literal inside a printed string).
