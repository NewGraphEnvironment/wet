## Findings

- **[bug]** `research/station_flow_departure.md:95`: "The short-record gauges score a median of 0.69 over the 12 with three or more years." Three paragraphs up the research defines the set as "13 have 3–10 complete windows", so all 13 have three or more years, and the sentence reads as excluding a gauge with a short record. The excluded gauge is 08JB006, which has 5 complete migration windows (report section 1) but only 2 years in common with its carried series (report section 3: `08JB006   2      -`). The criterion that computes the 12 is three or more years *both series have* (`carry_score()`, `length(yrs) < 3`), not three or more years of record. Fix: "over the 12 with three or more years in common with the carried series".

## What the round-4 enumeration missed, and why

The round-4 enumeration was bounded to "every section 4 and research sentence that quantifies over the extension or a span". It walked the series axis (statistic × window) only inside section 4 and the Maxan part of research. The Question 1 and Question 2 research paragraphs quote one series' score: findings 1-3 above. They do not quantify over the extension or a span, so they fell outside the boundary. Yet they carry the same defect: a label naming the window, or no series at all, beside a number from the migration or spawning **mean**. Finding 1 is the round-3 shape again, a count whose criterion (scored years in common) is not the criterion the label names (years of record). The enumeration listed the pool definitions and these numbers as checked, but it checked the **values**. It did not check that the noun each value is attached to is the object `carry_score()` computes it over. The termination this class needs: for every research sentence carrying a number, write the object the code computes it from (series, window, statistic, year set) next to the noun the sentence uses, and compare the two.
