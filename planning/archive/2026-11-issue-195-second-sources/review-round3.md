## Mechanism and its reach

**Mechanism.** The Tool registry stores values derived from other artifacts (the provincial days, the provincial registry, HYDAT, the bound, the Tool CSVs), and each consumer combines a stored value with a live copy of its source, trusting that the copy is the one the value was derived from. The registry names that source by a key coarser than the key the source itself is identified by. Round 1 was the producer and the consumer reading one fact two ways: the cut measured on complete days, then used as if it were the record's first hour. Round 2 was the registry not naming its source at all. Round 2's fix names the provincial source by its snapshot date (`bcenv_snapshot`). But the provincial days the cut was measured on are keyed in the cache by `days_<manifest md5>_v<wet_bcenv_reader>`, a finer key. So the runtime guard is under-keyed relative to the cache it stands for.

Two consumers combine the Tool registry with a provincial artifact: `wet_bcwatertool_station_days()` (via `wet_station_daily()`) and `wet_station_list()`. Both now call `wet_bcwatertool_against()` and `wet_registry_check()`. The harvest's tier (ii) calls `against()` too. `wet_bcwatertool_daily()` applies no cut, as documented, and its only callers are `wet_bcwatertool_station_days()` and the harvest.

## Findings

- **[severity: fragile]** R/wet_bcwatertool.R:231-239 (`wet_bcwatertool_against()`), R/wet_station_daily.R:171-186, inst/extdata/station_sources/bcwatertool_20261028_hydat.csv (`bcenv_snapshot`) — the runtime guard keys the cut's source on the snapshot date alone. data-raw lines 228-231 measure `continues_before` on `wet_bcenv_days()`, a file keyed by the provincial manifest's md5 and `wet_bcenv_reader`. R/wet_bcenv_daily.R:98-100 tells maintainers to bump that version whenever what is stored changes, for example a grade moved into or out of `wet_bcenv_grades$unusable` (R/wet_bcenv_daily.R:151 says to do exactly that on an unknown grade), or the local-standard-time offset. After such a bump, every stored `continues_before` describes days the reader no longer builds, and `wet_bcwatertool_against()` still passes, since the date is unchanged. If the provincial first usable day moves earlier than the stored cut, `wet_station_daily()` returns the Tool's day and the province's complete day for the same date under one station number. That is two rows for one station-day: the function's contract is one row per station-day, and nothing after the rbind (R/wet_station_daily.R:151-157) refuses it. `wet_window_stats()` and cd would then count that day twice. If the first day moves later, the Tool's days stop short of the record, leaving a silent gap. Today the harvest catches this, but only when someone runs it (`treg_cut`, scripts/station_sources_harvest.R:359-361). This is the round-2 defect one key-component over: the stale state is internally consistent, and the runtime guard reads a coarser grain than the property. Remedy: either stamp the registry with the provincial days key (manifest md5 prefix and `wet_bcenv_reader`) and compare it in `wet_bcwatertool_against()`, or refuse duplicated `station_number`/`date` pairs in `wet_station_daily()` after the rbind. The second is cheap, and it covers any future second publisher (wet#192) as well. Not live in the shipped data: the three cuts that add days, 2021-09-30, sit six years after those listings' last days in 2015, and Tofino's four add none.
