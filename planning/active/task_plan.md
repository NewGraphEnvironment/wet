# Task: PCIC per-year routed flow: keep the years, weight donors by modelled covariance, and score donors, PCIC and a blend at held-out gauges (#122)

PCIC's routed run (hydromosaic, Raven over VIC-GL, PNWNAmet daily 1950–2012) is daily and year by year, but fwapg's crosswalk reduces each batch with `cdo ymonmean` and deletes the daily file, so fwapg holds only climatologies: `pcic.discharge_monthly`, and `pcic.subbasin_daily` as 366 day-of-year bins. Change over time needs the years.

Component 3 of #118. Two halves, one in the fwapg fork (NewGraphEnvironment/fwapg#42) and one here. Depends on #120 (closed 2026-10-26) for the rule's shape and the first estimator.

## Phase 1: Register the rule (committed alone, before any candidate exists)

- [x] `scripts/departure_pcic.R`: header only, no body. It fixes, in this order: the pool; windows and statistics; how the modelled series are made; the candidates; the two-stage rule; scoring; the leak-free pool; the short-record test set; sensitivities (reported, unable to ship); the fwapg#42 interface and parity check (the amendable section); outputs; what it refuses.
  - **Pool, per (variable, period)** (G4): the shipped fit's calibration gauges (`cal`, HYDAT 20260717) whose snapped segment has a `pcic.segment_weights` row (the definition of "routed flow on the segment" for this script; 290 today, named once) and ≥ 10 complete window-years of that statistic in 1981–2010 (observed). The headline pool is the migration mean's. Donors and held-out targets both come from it. **One stations table for every run** (G7): the pool plus the short-record test set, so `wet_station_space()`'s scaling is the same in every leave-one-out, leak-free and test-set call. Predictors as #70/#102/#120: easting, northing (km, EPSG:3005) and log drainage area.
  - **Modelled series:** each gauge's segment routed day by day over 1951–2012 from the per-year product (`wet_routed_years()`, Phase 2), clamped at 0 per day and rounded to 5 decimals as fwapg rounds its published days (the parity check reads the unrounded sub-basin values, not these; A4); then `wet_window_stats()` on the same windows, giving a modelled window value per year. A modelled departure is `log(value / mean over 1981–2010)` of the modelled series (days for cov_day); the constant cancels in centred scoring, so PCIC's bias and baseline do not touch its score, which the header says. A modelled window-year ≤ 0 is dropped from logs and counted (zero-routed segments: 08JA015, 08JA016, 08JA014, 08JA028, 08DB013 route to about 0 in the routed report).
  - **Stage 1, donor weighting** (k = 5 as #120; candidates on the same k nearest in predictor space unless stated):
    - `distance` — the incumbent: `wet_departure_carry()` as shipped by #120.
    - `covariance` — the same k nearest, each weight multiplied by `max(r, 0)`, where r is the Pearson correlation, per (variable, period), of the target's and the donor's modelled log departures (days for cov_day) over the years both modelled series have; r needs at least 30 such years, and an r that is NA (a constant series) or rests on fewer is treated as 0 (G1, G6). A target whose k donors all get weight 0 falls back to `distance` for that target and is counted.
    - `covariance_pick` — the k donors with the highest r among the 15 nearest, weighted by `max(r, 0)` alone (no distance term); the same r rule, and the same fallback when fewer than one of the 15 has r > 0.
    - Rule: the candidate with the highest median per-gauge NSE ships if it beats `distance` by ≥ 0.01 (unrounded, 1e-9 allowed) under leave-one-gauge-out **and** in the leak-free pool; otherwise `distance`. A tie goes to the candidate listed first (G2). The header states the expected null (A1): PNWNAmet forcing is shared across a region, so modelled r among nearby segments is high and flat and `covariance` ≈ `distance` is the likely result; with 62 years r's sampling SD is about 0.13, so `covariance_pick` selects partly on noise, which is why a win must clear the leak-free pool too. The median distance of picked donors is reported.
  - **Stage 2, the competition** (on the stage-1 shipper):
    - `donors` — observed departures carried from donor gauges (stage-1 winner).
    - `pcic` — the target's own modelled departure at its segment.
    - `blend` — 0.5 · donors + 0.5 · pcic, in log units (days for cov_day); a year either component lacks is missing from `blend` (G3).
    - Rule: `pcic` or `blend` ships if its median per-gauge NSE beats `donors` by ≥ 0.01 under leave-one-gauge-out and in the leak-free pool; otherwise `donors`; between the two, the higher median, a tie to `pcic` (listed first). After 2012 only donors carry values whatever ships.
  - **Scoring, on common years** (B1): per held-out gauge, every candidate of a stage is scored on the same years: 1951–2012 ∩ the gauge's complete window-years ∩ the years every candidate of that stage has a value for (as #120's question 1 did). `wet:::wet_nse()` of the candidate against the observed log migration-window mean, **both centred on their mean over those years** (as #120's question 2, so no target baseline is needed and NSE 0 is "no departure"); a gauge with < 3 such years is not scored and counted. Median over gauges decides; quartiles reported. The report gives, per stage, the same n and the same years per gauge for every candidate (an assertion, not a sentence).
  - **Leak-free:** a held-out gauge's upstream and downstream pool gauges (`wet:::wet_fwa_upstream()` on `cal$wscode`/`localcode`, either direction; a co-located pair shares codes and is removed, counted) taken out of its donors, every candidate refitted. Runs always (cal carries the codes; no database needed).
  - **Short-record test set:** two sources, united: the stations rds `test` gauges (natural, 1–9 complete years in 1981–2010, already snapped) and the #120 box short-record gauges reproduced by the pilot's rule (natural BC gauges in the Bulkley box with 3–10 complete migration windows; 08EE018 among them, and in neither stations rds set, measured: its record predates the 1981–2010 selection window), snapped with `wet_station_snap()` through `WET_PG*`; kept where the snap is accepted, the segment has a weights row, and 3–10 complete migration-window years fall inside 1951–2012. Scored from the full pool by every candidate, separately, deciding nothing; 08EE018's row is named in the report (#130 reads it).
  - **Sensitivities, reported, unable to ship:** k = 3 and 8; spawning mean; migration and spawning min7 and cov_day; blend weights 0.25 and 0.75; PCIC's own skill (centred NSE and correlation, which differ where the amplitude is off) by `routed_basin()`, drainage-area class and `cal$nesting`; the count of gauges whose r rests on fewer than 62 years and the count where `covariance` fell back to `distance`.
  - **Refuses:** `WET_HYDAT_RELEASE` ≠ 20260717; `WET_FWAPG_COMMIT` unset; a dirty `R/` or `scripts/` tree (as `pcic_routed_compare.R`; AC3); the per-year product failing the parity check over every sub-basin the pool's and test set's segments name (AC2), or lacking a sidecar commit; a read sub-basin without 22,646 days; a pool under 100 gauges; a `cal` whose scores are stale (`shipped_heldout()`'s guards). No timestamp in the report, so a rerun is byte-identical.
