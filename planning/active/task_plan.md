# Task: Per-year ERA5-Land snow for a watershed, and the regression of the Maxan window series on peak SWE and melt date (#121)

Component 2 of #118. `wet_snow_normals()` averages cd's annual ERA5-Land snow layers over 1981–2010 onto a grid; nothing in wet returns those values per year, or for one watershed. The pilot (#120) left Maxan's year-to-year flow with explanations only in prose. Outcome: a function giving each year's `swe_max`, `snowmelt_doy_50` (and any other annual cd snow variable) for a watershed's polygons; a script regressing the Maxan extended series and the observed series at 08EE003 and 08EE013 on them, coefficients with intervals and residual error; a research section. Nothing is published; no fit is touched.

## What exploration found (2026-10-26)

- **cd's coverage is not a blocker.** Every annual snow layer in `cd::cd_catalog()` (cd 0.5.0; `swe_max`, `snowmelt_doy_50`, `snowmelt_rate_peak`, `snowmelt`, `swe`, `snowfall`, `snowfall_fraction`, `snow_cover`) has 76 bands named 1950–2025, 0.1°, extent −140.05…−113.95 × 47.95…60.05 (read from the remote headers, `CD_RUN_TIME 2026-10-10`, `CD_VERSION 0.6.3`). Over the pilot box (−127.6…−125.6 × 53.9…54.9) no band has an NA cell; domain-wide every band has the same 10,842 NA cells (sea). So `wet_ws_sample()`'s all-layers-at-once cover rule costs nothing here. cd's units: `swe_max` mm, `snowmelt_doy_50` day, `snowmelt_rate_peak` mm/wk (`cd/R/cd_variables.R`). In the pilot box `swe_max` peaks at 1,783 mm, so the 3,000 mm cap never binds there.
- **Reuse:** `wet_snow_normals()` (`R/wet_snow_normals.R`) has the catalog lookup, `cd_cache_fetch()`, band-year check, CRS check and `cap`; `wet_ws_sample(r, ws, "area")` (`R/wet_ws_sample.R`) gives exact-fraction cell means per polygon (planar fractions in the raster's own degrees, no `cellSize()`) with one `cover` across layers. The synthetic fixture `local_snow()` in `tests/testthat/test-wet_snow_normals.R` (one band per year, value = year − 1980, constant per band) carries over, with a variant whose values vary across cells.
- **Watershed polygons:** `wet_station_snap(conn, stations)` (`R/wet_station_snap.R`) snaps a HYDAT station to `watershed_feature_id`, `wscode`, `localcode`. On the local fwapg (`fresh-db`, localhost:5432, reachable now): 08EE003 → 7653689 (2,315 km², ~5,450 fundamental watersheds, 6.2 MB WKB), 08EE013 → 7619666 (567 km², ~1,005, 1.25 MB), 08EE018 → 9939958 (364 km², ~790, 0.9 MB), all `accepted`. Houston contains Buck and Maxan. Only 08EE013 is in `data/wb/stations_20260717.rds`; the other two are snapped in the script. The upstream set is the `FWA_Upstream` join on `fwa_watersheds_poly` restricted to `b.wscode_ltree <@ a.wscode_ltree` (as `scripts/upstream_area_check.R:69`).
- **The Maxan series is not stored.** `scripts/departure_pilot.R` (810 lines) rebuilds it inside the run (`mx[[paste(v, p)]]$e`, the extended series with `dep`, `donor`, `source` and `attr(, "donors")`; `$a`, the carried departures; lines ~674–730) and writes only the report (`writeLines(out, report)` at line 810). Its header (lines 12–14) says it writes the report "and nothing else", and its leak-free sensitivity connects with a bare `DBI::dbConnect(RPostgres::Postgres())`, i.e. libpq's `PG*` variables (line 325), unlike every other wet script (`WET_PG*`); the tracked report ran leak-free (49 of 53 snapped). The pilot runs in a few minutes.
- The package bundles spatial layers as xz-compressed rds, never GeoPackage (`data-raw/station_vignette_map.R:6-8`: a GeoPackage of the same layers is several times larger); `wet_ws_geom()` builds a SpatVector from WKT. `inst/extdata/` holds two small files. CI runs pkgdown only (`.github/workflows`), so examples run there against the installed package and no R CMD check does. `tests/testthat/helper-fwapg.R` has `local_fwapg()` for live tests.

## Decided at the gate

User, 2026-10-26: the plan as presented, parked. Mechanism, picked here (conventional defaults), revised where the Plan review (`review-plan.md`) showed the code contradicting it:

- **Function name and shape:** `wet_snow_annual(ws, vars = c("snowmelt_doy_50", "swe_max"), years = NULL, catalog = NULL, cap = c(swe_max = 3000))`, a sibling of `wet_snow_normals()` with no grid and no resampling. `ws` is a `SpatVector` of polygons (fundamental watersheds from fwapg, or one outline) for **one** watershed; the function dissolves them (`terra::aggregate()`) and samples the union once with `wet_ws_sample(method = "area")` over one stack of `<var>|<year>` layers, so fundamental watersheds and their union give identical results by construction (review item 1; `expanse()` weighting would agree only to ~1e-3 at 54°N). Nested or overlapping polygons dissolve harmlessly; passing several watersheds returns their union, which the roxygen says. `years = NULL` means every band. Returns `data.frame(year, <vars>, cover)`, one row per year. `cap` limits each cell's value in each year before sampling (the same 3,000 mm glacier cap as the normals). `wet_ws_sample()`'s rule holds: a cell counts only where every layer has a value, so a year's value can depend on `years` and `vars`; documented and tested.
- **The pilot saves its series:** `scripts/departure_pilot.R` additionally writes `data/departure/departure_pilot_<release>.rds` (gitignored): `mx` whole (all 8 statistic × window combos, `e` with its attributes and `a`), the pilot gauges' `wet_window_stats()` rows before `wsel()`'s `value > 0` filter, `release`, `ship`, `k_main`, `baseline`, `level`, and `code_md5 = tools::md5sum("scripts/departure_pilot.R")`. `saveRDS()` goes after `writeLines(out, report)` so no draw moves. Its header's "and nothing else" (lines 12–14) is amended in the same commit, as are the #120 research section's Produced-by line and `CLAUDE.md`'s pilot sentence. The tracked report must regenerate byte-identical, which needs fwapg reachable through the pilot's `PG*` variables (the leak-free lines).
- **Bundled data for the example:** Maxan only (the pilot site), under `inst/extdata/snow_pilot/`: its dissolved outline as WKT in an xz rds (`maxan_outline.rds`, BC Albers, with `station_number` and `watershed_feature_id`), unsimplified unless the bundle exceeds 500 KB (then simplified at 50 m as `station_map.rds` is, stated in `provenance.txt`); cd's three annual layers (`swe_max`, `snowmelt_doy_50`, `snowmelt_rate_peak`) cropped to the outline plus a 2-cell margin, bands named by year, `CD_*` tags kept; `catalog.csv` with relative `href`, which the example joins onto `system.file("extdata/snow_pilot", package = "wet")` (a non-http href must exist as a file). Built by `data-raw/snow_pilot_data.R` under `devtools::load_all()`, which asserts the crop equals the remote at every cell and measures the bundle.
- **Regression specification, registered in `scripts/departure_snow.R`'s header and committed alone before any body code** (the repo's practice; nothing is chosen, 24 fits are reported):
  - Series: (i) observed migration- and spawning-window means at 08EE003 and 08EE013 as log departure from the gauge's mean over its complete 1981–2010 window-years (at least 10; 08EE003 has 18, 1981–1998), from the rds's window stats (HYDAT 20260717, approved days, complete window-years as the pilot defines them); (ii) Maxan's extended series under the pilot's shipped route (`ship`, read from the rds, not assumed) as `e$dep`, taken from the rds: its 1981–2010 mean is over extended years only, Maxan's observed years being 1975–1979; (iii) Maxan's carried departures (`a$anomaly`). Log flow and its departure share slopes; the departure form is reported so the intercept is the departure at mean snow.
  - Predictors: that year's `swe_max` (per 100 mm) and `snowmelt_doy_50` (per 10 days), over the gauge's upstream fundamental watersheds by `wet_snow_annual()`; `snowmelt_rate_peak` reported per year, not in the model.
  - Model: OLS, both predictors and each alone, over every year 1950–2025 with a response and both predictors (no series reaches 2025; Maxan's extension ends 2024). Reported: coefficients, 90 % intervals (`confint(fit, level = 0.9)`, t), residual SE, R², n, lag-1 residual autocorrelation over consecutive-year pairs only (08EE003 has no complete window 1999–2010), the predictors' correlation; extended years before 1950 excluded and counted; donor per extended year listed. Each watershed's mean `swe_max` and `snowmelt_doy_50` for 2011–2025 against 1950–1980, beside the pilot's before/after. Expected counts the guards hold: Maxan 75 extended migration-mean years in 1950–2024 (the report's per-year table); the gauges' counts are their pool-table counts in the tracked pilot report less years before 1950.
  - Parity line: the 1981–2010 mean of the per-year values over each watershed against the shipped `wet_snow_normals()` grid (`data/snow/snow_19812010_*.tif`, keyed to the fit's grid, gitignored, local) sampled over the same polygons; the difference is bilinear resampling alone (the cap never binds in the box). When the file is absent the report says "parity not run" and the rest proceeds.
  - Connection: `WET_PG*` as every other wet script; the pilot's `PG*` divergence is noted in the header.
