How the plan carries 1(b), decided by the plan (mechanism):

- **The chain table is the Tool registry.** It holds 53 rows: the 46 Tool stations of their own, and the 7 listings at provincial sites with a `continues` column naming the provincial number (`NA` on the 46). The 7 are not stations of their own: `wet_station_list()` lists the 46, and a call naming `"4601"` gets no flow (it is the provincial number's).
- **The cut.** For a provincial number asked with `"bcwatertool"` in `sources`, the continuing listing's days dated before the provincial record begins come back under the provincial number, `source = "bcwatertool"`; all its days where the provincial files hold no hour of the station. The record begins on the first day the provincial files hold any usable hour of the station, complete or not, stored in the Tool registry as `continues_before` and held to the files by the harvest (code-check round 1: cut at the first *complete* day, Tofino's four listings, the same gauges, returned the province's own partial first day; cut at the first hour they add nothing). The cut is taken from the registry, not from the rows read, so it is the same whichever sources are asked. A continuing listing's registry `first` and `last` are the days it adds. The bound and status rules are the Tool's, the cap taken over the listing's whole record. A gap between the two (BCCF 2015–2021) is reported by `wet_daily_gaps()` as any gap between sources is.
- **`wet_station_list()`** gives a provincial station that a Tool listing continues, when both sources are listed, the earlier `first`, and appends to its `credit` and `licence` what the earlier days carry ("; days before <date>: <publisher's credit>", "; days before <date>: none stated").
- **The general rule.** `wet_station_daily()`'s docs state it once: a station's own source first, and a second publisher's listing at its site, named in a registry, only for the days before the first. #192 adds rows of another source to that rule.
