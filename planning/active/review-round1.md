- **[severity: fragile]** scripts/channel_scale_pilot.R:348-386 (`bulk()` / `get_batch()`) — a batch that answered 422 and was halved caches only its children (`.nc`) and, at the leaves, `.missing`; the parent itself leaves no marker. Every rerun therefore re-requests every 422'd parent and re-walks the halving tree over the network. The fetch log shows it: after the first pass, the reruns at 02:34-02:50 UTC (runs 4 and 5, whose reports are the "two runs from the cache" research/pcic_channel_scale.md cites) re-sent about 280 TaiESM1 requests each, plus PNWNAmet and GCM parents (1,474 logged 422s in all). Consequences: (1) the header's "a rerun from the cache prints the same report" and the write-up's "two runs from the cache" hold only while hydromosaic is reachable and answers as before; offline, a rerun retries with 30/60/90 s sleeps and stops at `bulk download failed`; (2) if hydromosaic later serves a parent batch (e.g. TaiESM1 on the coast), the parent's `.nc` is written beside its stale children, `files = list.files(dir, ...)` returns both, and the report's NetCDF count, md5-of-md5s and the fetch cost (filtered by `%in% used`) silently include the stale children. Write a marker for a halved parent (or record the leaf set) so a cached rerun sends nothing.
  - **Rule:** code-check.md "Written data outlives the fix"
