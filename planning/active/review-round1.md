## Findings

- **[bug]** vignettes/segment-discharge.Rmd:615-617 and :668 — the Bulkley prose says routed PCIC's error is "mostly because of the two smallest basins, where it is off by +196% and −86%", but `rt_worst` is the two largest |errors|, not the two smallest basins, and the guard only checks `area_km2 < 25`. The two smallest BULK basins are 08EE028 (9.8 km2, −86 %) and 08EE012 (13.3 km2, +35 %); the +196 % gauge, 08EE025, is 21.5 km2, the third smallest. The published sentence is false for the current data, and the guard that is meant to hold the prose to the data passes anyway. Either say "two of the smallest basins" (and guard `all(rank(gb$area_km2)[match(rt_worst$station_number, gb$station_number)] <= 3)`), or guard that `rt_worst` are the two smallest (which would fail now).

- **[bug: wrong attribution published]** data/checks/pcic_routed_compare_20260717.txt line 26 (written by scripts/pcic_routed_compare.R:207), research/water_balance_method.md section 0 ("Attribution: unresolved between wet's snap ... and fwapg's placement; not PCIC's model"), and vignettes/segment-discharge.Rmd:273-275 ("once the few gauges sitting on a side channel or a reservoir are set aside"). The deficit is therefore in PCIC's VIC-GL runoff over that basin (or its forcing/grid), not wet's snap or fwapg's placement. It is also one of the 7 gauges removed to get the 178 → 171 "tie" (25.0 % / 25.3 %), so the vignette's description of what was set aside is wrong for it. The numbers are unaffected; the "neither is PCIC's model error" / "not PCIC's model" claims and the vignette's characterisation are not. (08EE028 and 08DB014, also on edge_type 1000, have no annual-table value, so their attribution is genuinely unresolved, which the report's blanket sentence also overstates.)
