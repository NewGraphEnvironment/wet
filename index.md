# wet

Stream discharge for BC’s Freshwater Atlas: monthly and seasonal flow
per segment, historical and climate-scenario, from modelled runoff and
hydrometric stations

[Chinook flow at two
stations](https://github.com/NewGraphEnvironment/wet/blob/main/vignettes/station-flow.Rmd)
works through the station path on real data: a map of the stations and
their catchments, the last five years against 1981–2010 in the
open-water Chinook windows, the daily hydrograph, departure since 1981,
and trends.
